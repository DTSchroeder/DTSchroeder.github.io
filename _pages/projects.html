This page collects the formal research projects and project contexts I have worked in. They are ordered by their relationship to my current research interests: information integrity and AI-enabled influence operations first, followed by scalable graph and AI systems, and then data infrastructure and interoperability.

Information Integrity and AI-Enabled Influence

  • AI-enabled influence operations and malicious AI swarms
    2025 - present · SINTEF Digital, University of Oslo / BI Norwegian Business School, Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy, and collaborators
    Research initiative on how coordinated AI agents, cyborg propaganda, and synthetic consensus can affect democratic discourse. My work focuses on threat modeling, simulation, evaluation, and infrastructure concepts such as an AI Influence Observatory. Selected outputs include How malicious AI swarms can threaten democracy, IO Factory, and the EU DisinfoLab webinar.

    Collaborators include Jonas R. Kunst, Meeyoung ("Mia") Cha, and Wenchao Dong. The simulation work is supported by Signe Riemer-Sørensen in SINTEF's Analytics and Artificial Intelligence group.

  • UMOD - Understanding and Monitoring Digital Wildfires
    2017 - 2020 · Research Council of Norway SAMRISK II · Simula and NTNU
    PhD project on detecting, analyzing, and preparing for fast-spreading online misinformation. My dissertation and several publications grew out of this work on digital wildfires, misinformation propagation, and online social networks.

    My PhD supervisors were Odej Kao at the Technical University of Berlin and Johannes Langguth at Simula. I also collaborated with Carsten Griwodz, Pål Halvorsen, and Michael Riegler at Simula Metropolitan Center for Digital Engineering.

  • DD-MAC - Decoding Digital Media in African Regions of Conflict
    2021 - 2024 · Research Council of Norway · Project number 325123 · OsloMet, Simula, Utrecht University, African Studies Centre Leiden, Addis Ababa University, and Voice4Thought
    Interdisciplinary project on how digital communication shapes conflict development and mediation in sub-Saharan Africa. I focused on computational methods and social-media data, including empirical work on Ethiopia and Mali.

    I worked with Kristin Skare Orgeret, Bruce Mutsvairo, and Mirjam de Bruijn on disinformation, hate speech, diaspora dynamics, and the relationship between digital and traditional media.

Scalable Graph Computation and AI Systems

  • CEREBRA - A unified framework for trustworthy general-purpose AI
    2026 - 2030 · Horizon Europe Research and Innovation Action · Grant agreement 101299046
    Project developing an adaptive, explainable, and trustworthy European general-purpose AI platform. Within SINTEF's Smart Data work, my contribution is connected to knowledge graphs, semantic technologies, neuro-symbolic AI, uncertainty-aware reasoning, provenance, and trustworthy data pipelines, including pilots on maritime trajectory analysis and coastal resilience in the Oslo Fjord.
  • Graph-Massivizer - Extreme and Sustainable Graph Processing for Urgent Societal Challenges in Europe
    2023 - 2025 · Horizon Europe · Grant agreement 101093202
    Project on high-performance, scalable, and sustainable processing of extreme data through massive graph representations. I worked on scalable graph processing and reasoning with collaborators from the project consortium.

    My collaborators included Dumitru Roman, Alexandru Iosup, and Michael Cochez. We explored serverless computing for efficient massive-graph programming and processing.

  • EGNE - Enabling Graph Neural Networks at Exascale
    2020 - 2022 · Simula Research Laboratory and University of Bergen
    Project developing computational frameworks for scaling graph neural networks to large parallel systems. I contributed to this work during my postdoc at Simula Research Laboratory, extending deep learning methods to unstructured graph data.

    I worked with Johannes Langguth, Xing Cai, and Fredrik Manne.

  • enRichMyData - Enabling Data Enrichment Pipelines for AI-driven Business Products and Services
    2022 - 2025 · Horizon Europe · Grant agreement 101070284
    Project developing scalable data-enrichment pipelines for AI and big-data applications. In the SINTEF context, the work focused on data cleaning, linking, and scaling enrichment pipelines so that high-quality datasets can be built and used more effectively.

Data Infrastructure and Interoperability

I collaborate with Arne Jørgen Berre on data infrastructure and interoperability across these marine and environmental projects.

  • AquaINFRA - Infrastructure for Marine and Inland Water Research
    2023 - 2026 · Horizon Europe · Grant agreement 101094434
    Project building FAIR data and services, an interaction platform, and virtual research environments for marine and freshwater science. My contribution is in the Smart Data context around interoperable data infrastructure and cross-domain data services.
  • SEADITO - Social-Ecological Analysis and Models for Digital Twin Ocean
    2024 - 2027 · Horizon Europe · Grant agreement 101157243
    EU Mission Ocean project developing analytical methods, social-ecological models, and decision-support components for the European Digital Twin Ocean. My work is connected to SINTEF's data infrastructure and interoperability activities for marine and environmental domains.
  • Geo4Water - Geodata Space for Smart Water Monitoring & Response from Extreme Weather
    2025 - 2026 · Digital Europe Programme / DS4SSCC · Grant agreement 101123342
    Pilot project creating a geodata space for flood management, pollution monitoring, damage assessment, and urban resilience across Oslo, Donegal, San Javier, and Valencia. My work is connected to SINTEF's rollout and use of the data space for Norwegian stakeholders in the Oslo region.
