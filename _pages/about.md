I am a Research Scientist in the [Smart Data group at SINTEF](https://www.sintef.no/en/digital/departments/department-of-sustainable-communication-technologies/smart-data-research-group/), based in Oslo. I study information integrity and build methods for working with large, connected datasets.

My recent work asks how coordinated AI systems could manipulate public debate, and how we can simulate these campaigns to understand the risks and test possible defenses. I also work on scalable graph computing and data infrastructure for marine and environmental research.

## Background

Before joining SINTEF, I worked at OsloMet on digital communication and conflict in Ethiopia and Mali through DD-MAC, and as a postdoctoral researcher at Simula on scalable graph neural networks through EGNE. At SINTEF, I also contributed to the now-completed Graph-Massivizer project on processing very large graphs.

I received my PhD from the Technical University of Berlin, working with Simula on UMOD: understanding and monitoring "digital wildfires," the rapid spread of harmful misinformation online.
