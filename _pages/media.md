## Selected Highlights

- **World Economic Forum, June 2026:** The article is also cited in [Preparing for the Future: The Pillars of Digital Safety Foresight](https://www.weforum.org/publications/preparing-for-the-future-the-pillars-of-digital-safety-foresight/).

## For Media

Daniel Thilo Schroeder is a research scientist at SINTEF in Oslo. He studies how coordinated AI systems could manipulate public debate and develops methods to simulate and evaluate these risks.

**Topics:** AI-enabled disinformation, malicious AI swarms, cyborg propaganda, and information integrity.
