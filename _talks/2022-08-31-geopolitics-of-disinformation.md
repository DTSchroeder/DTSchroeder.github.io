Talk on conspiracy theory dynamics in social networks at the summer academy **The Geopolitics of Disinformation - Social Media and International Relations**, organised by the Academy of International Affairs NRW in Bonn.

The academy brought together researchers and experts from politics to discuss disinformation, propaganda, and the role of social media in international relations.

## Event

- Talk date: August 31, 2022
- Session: Working Group B
- Venue: Academy of International Affairs NRW, Rheinallee 24, Bonn, Germany
- Summer academy dates: August 29 - September 1, 2022
- Role: Speaker
