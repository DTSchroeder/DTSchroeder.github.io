Speaker at the CIENS AI Network (CAIN) seminar on generative AI and trust in research, representing SINTEF.

My talk, **AI and automated disinformation: What happens to trust?**, addressed how coordinated AI agents can manipulate public debate and erode trust in institutions, and the implications for research practice.

The seminar also explored trust in human-chatbot interactions and how narratives in chatbot conversations present AI as intelligent and reliable.
