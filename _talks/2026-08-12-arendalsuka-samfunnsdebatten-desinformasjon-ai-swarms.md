Talk and panel discussion at Arendalsuka 2026 on AI-enabled disinformation, synthetic consensus, and resilience against threats to democratic discourse.

## Topic

Bellona, World Expression Forum, and Utsyn gathered researchers, security authorities, business leaders, and politicians to discuss how disinformation and AI swarms can fabricate false public consensus, manipulate public debate, and challenge democratic resilience.

Daniel Thilo Schroeder contributed the research segment "Fra boter til AI-svermer: En ny æra for påvirkningsoperasjoner" on the shift from bot-driven influence operations to coordinated AI swarms.
